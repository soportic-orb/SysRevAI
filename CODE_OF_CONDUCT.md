# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
SysRevAI community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity. We commit to acting in ways
that foster an open, diverse, and healthy community.

## Our standards

We expect everyone to:

- Be kind, patient, and respectful toward others.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and give and accept constructive feedback
  gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Prioritize what is best for the overall community.

Behavior that harms or excludes others — including harassment, personal
attacks, or any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting — is not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at
**octavirodriguezblanco@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is obligated to respect the
privacy and security of the reporter.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's "Enforcement Guidelines"
(correction → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban) as described in the
linked document.
